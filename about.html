Our Mission

Founded in 1987, the Rickology Research Institute is dedicated to the study of emotional permanence and the psychological foundations of commitment. Our flagship research focuses on the "Astley Commitment Model," which explores why individuals choosing stability over transience exhibit higher levels of cognitive fulfillment.

Global Reach

With research facilities in London, New York, and Tokyo, our team of psychometricians works tirelessly to develop assessment tools that accurately map the human experience.
